Toward Rapid Flood Mapping Anywhere via Terrain- and Land-Cover-Conditioned Optical–SAR Fusion

Jiepan Li, He Huang, Wenke Li, Linxin Li, Anqi Xie, Ruoru Ye, Lei Hu, Ting Hu, Wei He, and Liangpei Zhang

Remote Sensing of Environment (RSE)

Under Revision · 2026Code ↗Dataset ↗
Overview

To answer whether observed optical–SAR evidence indicates flooding within local terrain and land-cover contexts, we introduce GeoFlood-275, a global, event-level benchmark for terrain- and land-cover-conditioned rapid flood inundation mapping. Additionally, we propose GeoFloodNet, a novel optical–SAR fusion framework tailored for context-aware rapid flood mapping.
